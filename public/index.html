About us

We teach skills that pay.

Learnify is an online platform for practical digital skills. We teach what businesses actually pay for, then help you turn it into freelancing work or your own business.

Our mission

Most students finish college with theory but no earning skill. Learnify fixes that with live classes, real tools and hands-on homework, taught in simple Hinglish.

Every course follows one path: learn the skill, practise it on real work, grow a portfolio, and start earning through freelancing, clients or your own business.
